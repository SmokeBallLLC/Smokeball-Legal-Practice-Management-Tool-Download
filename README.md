## 🧠 Overview
**Smokeball** is an all-in-one practice management platform tailored specifically for modern law firms. It combines case tracking, document automation, and client management tools into a single, user-friendly interface, helping legal professionals manage their workload efficiently while maintaining high standards of service.  

Designed for law practices of any size, Smokeball allows teams to streamline routine tasks, organize case files, and maintain accurate billing and time tracking records. Its integration with Microsoft Office and cloud storage ensures that documents and data are always accessible and secure. Reporting and analytics tools provide actionable insights to help firms improve productivity, optimize workflows, and make data-driven decisions. With Smokeball, law offices gain a flexible, reliable, and professional-grade solution that supports both day-to-day operations and long-term growth.
